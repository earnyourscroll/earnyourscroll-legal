01 Overview

EarnYourScroll ("EYS," "we," "us") is an Android app that helps you build a healthier relationship with your phone: it asks for a short physical exercise — pushups, squats, a timed plank, or a mindful breathing break — before letting you open apps you've chosen to restrict. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can control or delete it.

We built EarnYourScroll to require as little personal information as possible. There are no ads and no third-party trackers in the app today. If that changes, we'll update this policy and the in-app notice before it happens.

02 Information we collect

Account information
If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google to create your account. You can also use the app anonymously without creating an account — we then assign a random, non-identifying device ID.
Onboarding answers
The first name you enter, your companion character choice, your stated daily screen-time estimate, and your stated goal/aspiration.
Restricted apps list
Which apps on your device you've chosen to restrict (e.g. Instagram, TikTok), and any feature-level settings you pick for them.
App usage data (optional)
If you grant the optional Usage Access permission, the app reads how much time you've recently spent in each app directly from Android, to suggest which apps to restrict. This number is read live from your device each time it's needed and is never sent to our servers or stored in your account.
Workout history
A record of exercises you complete — type, rep count, duration, and timestamp — and the "Edge" coins you earn from them.
Push notification token
A device token used to send you reminder notifications. We don't use this for anything else.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, messages, or browsing content from other apps.

03 Camera & exercise tracking

For pushups and squats, EarnYourScroll uses your device's camera to count your reps through on-device pose detection (Google ML Kit). This processing happens entirely on your phone:

  • Camera frames are analyzed in memory, one at a time, and discarded immediately after each frame is processed.
  • No photo, video, or image frame is ever saved to your device's storage, uploaded, or transmitted anywhere.
  • Only the result of that processing — a numeric rep count — is saved as part of your workout history.
We verified this directly in the app's code: the exercise-tracking screens only use CameraX's live-preview and frame-analysis features, with no capture or recording feature wired up anywhere, and every frame is explicitly released right after it's analyzed.

04 Accessibility permission

EarnYourScroll asks for Android's Accessibility Service permission to detect the single moment when you open an app you've chosen to restrict, so it can show the exercise prompt. This permission is used for that purpose only:

  • We don't read, log, or store the content of your screen, your keystrokes, or any other app's data.
  • The service only checks which app package has come to the foreground — nothing else.

05 How we use your information

  • To run the core feature: detecting a restricted app and unlocking it once you've completed an exercise.
  • To track your streaks, Edge coin balance, and progress over time, and show them back to you.
  • To keep your data in sync if you use the app on more than one device.
  • To send you reminder notifications, if you've allowed them.
  • To fix bugs and improve the app.

We do not use your information to serve ads, and we do not sell your information.

06 How we share information

We don't sell or rent your information. We share it only with the service providers that make the app work, each bound to process it only on our behalf:

Supabase
Our backend database and authentication provider. Stores your account, workout history, and restricted-apps list.
Google Sign-In
If you choose to sign in with Google, Google processes that authentication. See Google's own privacy policy for how they handle it.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of EarnYourScroll or our users.

07 Data storage & security

Your account data is stored with Supabase, encrypted in transit over HTTPS. Database access is restricted with row-level security rules so that your data is readable only by your own authenticated account — we tested this directly, including attempts to read one account's data using another account's credentials, and confirmed it's blocked.

08 Data retention & deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete Account → type DELETE to confirm.
  • By email: contact us at the address below and we'll delete it for you.

Deletion removes your profile, workout history, and restricted-apps list from our systems. This is permanent and can't be undone.

09 Children's privacy

EarnYourScroll is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10 Your choices & rights

  • You can use the app anonymously, without a Google account.
  • Usage Access and notification permissions are optional and can be turned off in your device's system settings at any time.
  • You can request a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us.
  • You can revoke the Accessibility permission at any time in Android Settings — EYS will simply stop detecting restricted apps until it's re-granted.
