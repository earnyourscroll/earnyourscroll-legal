Account Deletion

EarnYourScroll

Last updated: October 26, 2026

You can permanently delete your EarnYourScroll account and all the data tied to it at any time, in two ways.

Option 1 — In the app

  1. Open EarnYourScroll and go to Settings
  2. Tap Delete Account
  3. Type DELETE in the confirmation field
  4. Tap Delete my account, then confirm on the final dialog

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Option 2 — By email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address associated with your account and we'll delete it for you within a few business days.

earnyourscroll@gmail.com

What gets deleted

Account & profile
Your sign-in record, display name, and companion selection — deleted.
Workout history
All logged exercises, rep counts, and Edge coin balance — deleted.
Restricted apps list
Which apps you chose to restrict and their settings — deleted.
This is permanent. Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered or restored, and a new account starts from zero.

Timing

In-app deletion is immediate. Email requests are processed within a few business days of us confirming it's really you.

EarnYourScroll — this page is the account-deletion path required by Google Play, linked from the app's Store listing and in-app Settings.
